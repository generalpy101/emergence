MG Motor UK → Shanghai SASAC

MG Motor UK Ltd sits three hops below SAIC Motor Corporation Limited through SAIC Motor International UK Ltd (formerly Nanjing Automobile Corporation (UK) Limited); SAIC Group holds 63.27% of it as at 31 December 2024 and is a state owned enterprise supervised by Shanghai SASAC.

Google LLC → Alphabet Inc.

Google LLC sits under XXVI Holdings Inc., the intermediate holding entity beneath Alphabet Inc., with international subsidiaries hanging off the operating company.

TechTarget → Informa PLC (Dec 2024)

Informa PLC beneficially owns 58.27% of TechTarget following a transaction that closed on 2 December 2024, held through Informa US Holdings Limited; TechTarget itself wholly owns BrightTALK, Industry Dive, NetLine, and the Canalys group.

Demandforce → KKR & Co. (+ Temasek 20%)

Demandforce sits under Internet Brands, held through a ten-entity KKR fund chain up to KKR & Co. Inc., with Temasek holding 20% of the aggregator alongside.
